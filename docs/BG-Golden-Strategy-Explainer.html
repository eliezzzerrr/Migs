The 6 mechanical steps

1

Mark structure (swings)

Pivot highs/lows are detected with swing strength = 1. The most recent swing low becomes the liquidity level the market must break to confirm a bearish shift.

2

Spot the Order Block

The last bullish (up) candle before the drop is tagged as the supply Order Block. This is "where smart money sold." Its high/low define the zone. (For BUY: last bearish candle before an up-impulse.)

3

Break of Structure (BOS)

A candle closes below the swing low → bearish BOS confirmed. The drop is the impulse leg. BOS stays "active" for Max BOS age = 30 bars.

4

Wait for the retrace

Price must pull back up into the OB, within Entry × ATR = 0.9 of the zone edge. No retrace = no trade. This is the patience filter that drives the win rate.

5

Fire + place the stop

On a confirmed bar touching the OB, the SELL fires. SL sits just beyond the OB (+0.1×ATR, min $9). The entry→SL distance = 1R, the unit everything else is measured in.

6

Targets at 1R / 2R / 3R

TP1=+1R, TP2=+2R, TP3=+3R. Full position rides to the highest enabled TP (currently TP2 = +2R final). Optional trails (BE-after-TP1 etc.) are off by default.
